summary: "Farm Buddy is a farmers-first advisory tool. A farmer describes a problem by typing, uploading a photo, opening the camera, or recording a voice note, and gets back a diagnosis of the plant, an assessment of the soil, and what to do about both."
problem: "Agricultural extension officers are the people who look at a sick crop and tell a farmer what to do about it. There are nowhere near enough of them to reach every farm, and the farmers who most need one are often the least able to travel to find one, or to describe the problem in written English. The expertise exists. The distribution does not."
problemTags:
  - "Expertise that cannot scale"
  - "Language and literacy barriers"
  - "Diagnosis is inherently visual"
approach:
  - title: "The camera is the primary input"
    body: "A photograph of the affected plant carries more diagnostic signal than a paragraph describing it, and it is far easier for the farmer to produce. Live capture and upload are both first-class, not an afterthought behind a text box."
  - title: "Voice counts as a description"
    body: "Requiring a typed description in English excludes the people the tool exists for. A recorded voice note is accepted as a full input, so a farmer can simply say what they are seeing."
  - title: "Answer with actions, not a label"
    body: "A diagnosis on its own changes nothing in a field. Every response returns the plant condition, an assessment of visible soil condition, and concrete recommendations for improving each."
